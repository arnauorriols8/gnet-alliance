About G Net

The first global alliance of pharmaceutical leaders

Industrial Sphere
Direct relationships with 95% of the world's major pharmaceutical corporations and their C-suite leadership.
Academic Sphere
Collaboration with leading research universities and medical institutions across all continents.
Institutional Sphere
Access to the WHO, FDA, EMA, and the most influential public and private regulatory bodies globally.
Alliance Networks
Active agreements with the most prominent associations in the global Life Sciences sector.

G Net is an exclusive, invitation-only body that convenes the chief executives and policy directors of the world's largest pharmaceutical corporations. Modelled on the governance architecture of the G7 and G20, G Net operates as a non-governmental alliance with direct access to the highest levels of international regulatory and political institutions.

Founded on the principle that the pharmaceutical industry requires a unified and powerful voice, G Net coordinates global strategy, facilitates high-level diplomatic engagement, and drives regulatory harmonisation across all member jurisdictions.

We are the nexus between the pharmaceutical industry, academia, public institutions, and the governments that shape the future of global health.

Political Influence
Direct engagement with heads of state and international regulatory bodies worldwide.
Strategic Alignment
Coordinated positions on global health policy and market access frameworks.
Regulatory Access
Priority channel to WHO, FDA, EMA and multilateral institutions globally.
Intelligence
Confidential geopolitical briefings and policy foresight for member CEOs.

Membership

Integrate into
this elite community

G Net membership is extended by invitation only, subject to peer review by the G Net Council. Each tier reflects a distinct level of engagement, access, and organisational commitment to the Alliance.

01
Associate Member
Associate

Entry-level access for organisations meeting minimum revenue and policy engagement criteria, with full access to the G Net ecosystem.

  • G Net Annual Summit access
  • Quarterly intelligence briefings
  • Working group participation
  • G Net policy digest
  • Intranet & community access
02
External Member
External

Companies without on-site presence that wish to participate in G Net services and benefit from the Alliance's global positioning.

  • Full service access remotely
  • Bilateral regulatory facilitation
  • Event & congress participation
  • Relational marketing inclusion
  • Investor & M&A ecosystem
03
Core Member — Voting
Member

Full membership with voting rights on all G Net resolutions, strategic mandates, and direct access to Council-level sessions.

  • Voting rights on all resolutions
  • Direct Council session access
  • CEO-level diplomatic introductions
  • Confidential geopolitical briefings
  • Priority regulatory channel
  • Heads-of-state engagement
04
Tier IV — Founding
Founding

Reserved for the original architects of G Net and organisations of exceptional global influence and market leadership.

  • Seat on the G Net Council
  • Co-authorship of annual mandate
  • Private heads-of-state meetings
  • Named in G Net Charter
  • Priority access — all services
  • G Net Founding Member seal
